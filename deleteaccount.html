Delete your Dragonfly account

This page explains how to ask Grand Illusion Entertainment to delete your Dragonfly account and the data connected to it.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app

Open Dragonfly, go to Account (in the footer), then tap Delete account.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to grandillusione@gmail.com with the subject Dragonfly account deletion request.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Dragonfly. That is how we confirm the account is yours.
  3. We will confirm by reply and delete your account and data within 30 days.
Write it in Gmail

What we delete

  • Your email address and account record.
  • Your saved sessions and game history.
  • Your device identifier and any active login sessions.
  • Your subscription status record.

Subscriptions

  • Bought on the web (Stripe): deleting your account cancels the subscription. No further charges will be made.
  • Bought through Google Play: cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments and subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription, and we cannot cancel it for you.

What we may keep

  • If you made a purchase, the payment provider (Google Play or Stripe) keeps transaction records for as long as the law and tax rules require. We do not store your card number.
  • Backups and server logs may hold your data for up to 30 additional days before they are overwritten.

Deleting only some data

If you only want certain data removed, such as your saved sessions, say so in your email and we will delete just that.

Read our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle your information.
