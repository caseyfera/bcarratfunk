Delete your Dragonfly account

This page explains how to ask Grand Illusion Entertainment to delete your Dragonfly account and the data connected to it.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app

Open Dragonfly, tap Account at the bottom of the screen, then tap Delete account.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to grandillusione@gmail.com with the subject Dragonfly account deletion request.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Dragonfly. That is how we confirm the account is yours.
  3. We will confirm by reply and delete your account and data within 30 days.
